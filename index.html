SweepKit transitions in your BPM and key

Pack risers and impacts are printed at someone else's tempo and key, so they smear when stretched and land off the downbeat. SweepKit builds the transition for your song: set BPM, key and chords, pick a transition, hear it land, step through variations, drag the WAV + MIDI into bar 1.

iOS: the silent switch mutes web audio. Turn it off if you hear nothing.
